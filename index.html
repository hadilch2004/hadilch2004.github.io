A mobile-first social app for university students: a live campus map with friends, hangouts with group chat, and club communities. Privacy is enforced by the database itself: ghost mode by default, location visible only to mutual friends and expiring automatically, and silent account deletion. Installable on iPhone and Android as a web app, deployed on Cloudflare Pages with a Supabase backend, and covered by 303 automated tests that include attempts to break the security rules.
